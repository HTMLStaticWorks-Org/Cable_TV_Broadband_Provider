Help & Answers

Questions about our plans?

Everything you need to know before joining ConnectWave.

Is there an installation charge for new connections?

Standard installation is completely FREE when you subscribe to any 6-month or 12-month billing plan. On monthly plans, a nominal one-time technician fee of ₹500 covers indoor optical cabling and router configuration.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Yes! Subscribers can change their plan at any time through the Subscriber Dashboard with pro-rata billing adjustments. Upgrades take effect within 15 minutes.

What equipment is provided with the connection?

We provide a state-of-the-art Optical Network Terminal (ONT) Wi-Fi 6 router. For combo plans, a 4K hybrid cable set-top box is also provided on a security deposit basis.

Are upload and download speeds symmetrical?

Yes, all ConnectWave pure fiber broadband connections deliver equal upload and download speeds, making high-resolution video calls, online backups, and gaming lag-free.
