Instant Solutions

Frequently asked questions

Helpful information for new and existing subscribers.

How do I pay my broadband or cable TV bill?

Sign in to your Subscriber Dashboard, view your current statement under "Pay Bills & Invoices", and pay instantly via UPI (GPay/PhonePe), Credit/Debit Card, or NetBanking.

What should I do if my internet stops working?

First, check that the fiber optical patch cord is firmly plugged into your ONT router and that the 'PON' light is steady green. If the 'LOS' light is blinking red, please raise a complaint via the Dashboard for immediate field technician dispatch.

How do I request a new connection?

Visit the Sign Up page, select your desired broadband and TV combo, and enter your delivery address. Our verification team will contact you within 2 hours.

Can I upgrade or change my plan anytime?

Yes! Upgrades are processed instantly in your Subscriber Dashboard under the "My Plan & Add-ons" tab with automated pro-rated billing.
