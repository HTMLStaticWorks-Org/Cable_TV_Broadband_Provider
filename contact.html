Common Troubleshooting Questions

What should I do if my internet is slow or not working?

Ensure the optical patch cord is firmly plugged into the green 'PON' port on your Wi-Fi 6 router. If the 'LOS' indicator light is blinking red, please raise a ticket via the Dashboard or call 1800-555-0199 for field engineer dispatch.

How can I change my Wi-Fi password?

Open your browser and navigate to 192.168.1.1, sign in with your default router credentials (printed under the ONT router), and update your SSID and WPA3 security key.

How do I upgrade or modify my television channels?

Log in to the Subscriber Dashboard, navigate to "My Plan & Add-ons", and select additional sports, movie, or regional language bouquets in 1 click.
