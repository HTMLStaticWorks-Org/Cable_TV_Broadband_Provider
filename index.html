Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before connecting.

How fast can I get a new connection installed?

In all active fiber zones, standard installation is completed within 24 to 48 hours of booking. Our field engineer brings the pre-configured Wi-Fi 6 ONT router and set-top box, performs optical splicing, and tests bandwidth in your presence.

Is standard installation really free?

Yes! Free installation applies when you subscribe to any 6-month or 12-month billing plan. On monthly plans, a nominal one-time technician setup fee of ₹500 covers indoor cabling and optical router setup.

Can I keep my existing television and cables?

Yes. Our 4K digital cable set-top box connects seamlessly to any television via standard HDMI or AV cables. We also provide full backward compatibility with non-smart TVs.

How do I pay my bill or raise a complaint?

You can pay bills in 1 click or raise a service ticket through the Subscriber Dashboard using UPI, Cards, or NetBanking with zero transaction charges.
