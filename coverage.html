How our pure fiber reaches your home

ConnectWave operates a carrier-neutral, triple-redundant underground ring topology. This means zero overhead wire clutter and unmatched weather resistance.

  • Direct FTTH (Fiber-to-the-Home): 100% optical fiber from distribution exchange to your living room.
  • Sub-8ms Edge Latency: Local peering with Google, Netflix, AWS, and Cloudflare nodes.
  • Battery-Backed Power Nodes: Your internet keeps running even during temporary neighborhood power outages.
  • 4-Hour MTTR: Field technicians on standby in every active sector.
